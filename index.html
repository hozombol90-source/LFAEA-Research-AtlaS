REPRODUCIBILITY · EPISTEMIC CONTROL · 06

Method & quality.

The atlas is designed as a research instrument: every claim is classified, every comparison has gates, and every missing field has a typed state.

01 · EVIDENCE STRENGTH

Four evidence grades

A
Direct published result

Peer-reviewed paper or final NASA report presenting a direct result. Display conditions, methods, and citation.

B
Official investigation context

NASA investigation page, engineering requirements, interim report, or supporting metadata. Do not present as a peer-reviewed conclusion.

C
Traceable reanalysis

Derived or reprocessed result from an uploaded dataset. Show method, script version, original file, and limitations.

D
Interpretation / hypothesis

Physical interpretation or proposed design concept not directly measured. Label it as an interpretation, not an experimental finding.

Evidence grade describes evidence type, not certainty. A grade-A paper can still have narrow conditions and measurement uncertainty.

02 · PRIORITISATION MODEL

Explainable ranking

Scenario relevance60%
Evidence type20%
Data coverage15%
Metadata completeness5%
Priority = 0.60R + 0.20E + 0.15D + 0.05M

R, E, D, and M are normalised editorial inputs. A score is a triage aid, not a model output, flammability limit, or safety risk.

04 · DATA LINEAGE

Typed missingness and provenance

Not measured

The experiment did not record the variable.

Not uploaded

The data may exist but was absent from the supplied package.

Not defined

A column / channel has no verified measurand or unit.

Not calibrated

A conversion cannot be reproduced from the available calibration package.

Conflicting labels

Two source files disagree; both descriptions remain preserved.

Not computable

The requested derived value is withheld until required inputs are available.

SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

What the atlas can responsibly claim

It can organise published findings, compare documented conditions, compute descriptive statistics from available files, and identify where evidence is incomplete. It cannot certify a material, predict a crewed-spacecraft emergency, or replace vehicle-specific procedures and engineering authority.
